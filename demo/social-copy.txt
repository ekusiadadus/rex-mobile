Hey everyone! I’ve been trying Rex and really enjoying it. I couldn’t wait for the official iOS client, so I built a small, unofficial browser client for my existing Rex sessions.

It uses Ghostty compiled to WebAssembly via coder/ghostty-web to render the terminal in the browser, with access over Tailscale. I can launch Claude and interact with the same session from Safari. The demo shows Safari and Rex side by side.

It’s an early personal project: VT snapshots sync about every two seconds, rather than streaming continuously. The demo is recorded on Mac Safari; iPhone IME, rotation, and resume still need device testing for this release.

Source: https://github.com/ekusiadadus/rex-mobile
Demo: https://github.com/ekusiadadus/rex-mobile/releases/tag/v0.1.0
