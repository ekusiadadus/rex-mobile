Hey everyone! I’ve been trying Rex and really enjoying it. I couldn’t wait for the official iOS client, so I built my own unofficial browser client!

It uses Ghostty compiled to WebAssembly via coder/ghostty-web to render my existing Rex sessions in the browser, with access over Tailscale. I can launch Claude, send prompts, and get replies from Safari on my iPhone 15 Pro Max. The video shows Safari and Rex displaying the same session side by side.

It’s still an early personal project: VT snapshots sync about every two seconds, and IME, rotation, and background/resume behavior need more testing. Feedback is welcome!

Source: https://github.com/ekusiadadus/rex-mobile
Demo: https://github.com/ekusiadadus/rex-mobile/releases/tag/v0.1.0
